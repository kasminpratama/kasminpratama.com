Starlink & Internet Connectivity.

Professional focus on the practical deployment of internet connectivity solutions, including Starlink systems and their integration with supporting network infrastructure.

OVERVIEW

Connectivity requires more than an internet connection.

Internet connectivity is part of a broader technical environment. A practical deployment requires appropriate equipment placement, physical installation, system configuration, network integration, and verification of connectivity.

My professional focus is centered on the practical implementation of connectivity solutions and the supporting network infrastructure required to distribute and use that connection within a technology environment.
