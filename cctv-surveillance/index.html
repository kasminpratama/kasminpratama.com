01 / PROFESSIONAL EXPERTISE

CCTV & Surveillance Systems.

Professional focus on the practical implementation, configuration, and supporting infrastructure of CCTV and surveillance systems.

OVERVIEW

Practical approach to surveillance systems.

CCTV systems are not limited to cameras and recording devices. A reliable surveillance environment requires proper installation, device configuration, recording management, network connectivity, and systematic testing.

My professional focus is centered on the practical technical processes involved in implementing and supporting surveillance systems.
