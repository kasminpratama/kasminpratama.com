02 / PROFESSIONAL EXPERTISE

Network Infrastructure.

Based in Kota Baubau, Sulawesi Tenggara, I work with network infrastructure that connects devices, services, and technology systems in the field — from physical installation and configuration to integration and troubleshooting.

OVERVIEW

Networks are built to work, not simply to connect.

A network can look simple on paper and still become difficult to manage once it is installed. Cabling, addressing, routing, switching, wireless access, and the devices connected to them all have to work together.

My work is focused on the practical side of that process: preparing the infrastructure, configuring network equipment, connecting systems, testing communication, and tracing problems when something does not work as expected.

HOW I APPROACH AN INSTALLATION

Start with the system, then work through the details.

01

Understand

Identify what needs to be connected, how the network will be used, and what infrastructure is already available.

02

NETWORK ENVIRONMENT

The parts I consider when working on a network.

Network problems are not always caused by the device that appears to be offline. I look at the surrounding infrastructure as well — the connection, addressing, switching, routing, wireless access, and physical installation.

01

02

03

Wireless Access

Wireless access points and their connection to the wired network, considered as part of the same network environment.

IP & Device Connectivity

Addressing and device communication needed for network equipment and connected systems to reach one another correctly.

05

Cabling & Physical Infrastructure

The cabling, power, equipment placement, and physical details that provide the foundation for the network to operate reliably.

SYSTEM INTEGRATION

Where the network becomes part of a larger system.

In the field, a network is often not the final objective. It is the infrastructure that allows other systems to communicate. CCTV, internet connectivity, wireless access, and other network-enabled equipment depend on that underlying connection.

This is why I approach network work together with the systems it is supporting. A connection is only useful when the devices on both ends can communicate as intended.
